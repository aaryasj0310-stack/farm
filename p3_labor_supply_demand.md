# Kaggriculture P3.3 — Labor Supply vs. Labor Demand Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary
This document analyzes the daily balance between **Labor Supply** (effective worker actions generated by the 13-unit workforce) and **Valuable Labor Demand** (the sum of all economically profitable farm tasks required each day) across the 30-day season.

Data is aggregated from the 100-game audit of the Promoted P2.3 Production Baseline (`536f1e7`).

---

## 2. Daily Labor Supply vs. Demand Ledger

- **Available Actions**: Exactly $(1 + \text{hands}) \times 24$ turns per day (minus turn-0 hire lag where hands first act at hour 1).
- **Valuable Demand**: Sum of mandatory crop watering, mandatory feed, urgent harvests, seed plantings, animal care passes, and necessary transit to execute them.
- **Shortfall**: Days where valuable demand exceeds productive throughput after necessary travel.

## 3. Peak Labor Days and Hourly Contention Profile

### The Two Major Bottleneck Epochs
1. **Days 3–5 (Early Core Bottleneck)**:
   - Available workforce is only **4 hands (5 active units = 120 turns/day)**.
   - Farm demand spikes as the first tranche of 8 Wheat tiles reaches maturity (Day 4), NE unlocks (Day 3–5), and initial strawberries are planted.
   - 5 units cannot physically water 16 tiles, harvest 8 ripe wheat, and stage feed.
   - Result: Day 4–5 wheat suffers decay and delayed replanting.
2. **Days 12–14 and Days 20–24 (Strawberry Harvest Peaks)**:
   - Workforce is at full capacity (12 hands = 312 turns/day).
   - Demand spikes to 340+ turns because 16–20 ongoing Strawberry tiles yield simultaneously, while 10 animals require mandatory daily feeding and care.
   - Because `PRIORITY_STANDARD_HARVEST = 65` sits below `PRIORITY_BONUS_WATER = 70`, workers spend turns watering wheat while ripe strawberries sit unharvested, delaying the next flowering tick!

### Hourly Task Contention Within Days
- **Hours 0–3 (Morning Rush)**: Mandatory daily feeding (85) and survival watering (100) saturate 100% of workers.
- **Hours 4–11 (Midday Dispatch)**: Bonus watering (70) and fertilizer collection (75) consume available workers. Standard harvest (65) and animal care (65) are consistently deferred.
- **Hours 12–19 (Evening Backlog)**: Workers attempt to clear the harvest and care backlogs, but travel distances to the shed exhaust the remaining turns.
- **Hours 20–23 (Night Cutoff)**: Uncompleted bonus watering passes expire without yield bonus; crops that missed harvest decay at hour 23.
